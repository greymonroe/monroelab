        Most graduate students and postdocs join the lab with support from external fellowships, and the lab has a strong track record of winning them — current lab members are funded by HFSP and BARD postdoctoral fellowships, among others.

        If you are interested in joining us, look through the programs below and email Grey ([gmonroe@ucdavis.edu](mailto:gmonroe@ucdavis.edu)) about which might fit you. **Fellowship applications from this lab are a team effort.** We usually start with an email conversation about research ideas, then develop the proposal together — you are not expected to write an application on your own.

        Deadlines, amounts, and eligibility change every cycle, so always confirm details on the program's own site.

        ---

        ## Postdoctoral Fellowships — International Applicants

        These programs bring international researchers to US labs. This is how most international postdocs join us.

        | Program | Who can apply | Support | Application |
        |---|---|---|---|
        | [HFSP Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://www.hfsp.org/funding/hfsp-funding/postdoctoral-fellowships) | Any nationality; PhD within the last ~3 years; you must move to a new country and propose work different from your PhD | 3 years of salary and research support | One competition per year, two stages: a short letter of intent, then an invited full proposal |
        | [EMBO Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://www.embo.org/funding/fellowships-grants-and-career-support/postdoctoral-fellowships/) | Researchers connected to EMBC member or partner states (by nationality or PhD country); PhD within the last 2 years | Up to 2 years of stipend | Two evaluation rounds per year |
        | [MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships (Global)](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships) | Nationals or long-term residents of EU / Horizon-associated countries | 12–24 months in our lab plus a 12-month return phase in Europe | One call per year (the 2026 call closes September 9) |
        | [Life Sciences Research Foundation](https://lsrf.org/) | Any nationality with a US host lab | 3 years | One cycle per year, historically due October 1 |

        Many countries also fund their own researchers to do a postdoc abroad — check your national funder. Examples: [JSPS Overseas Research Fellowships](https://www.jsps.go.jp/english/e-ab/) (Japan), [DFG Walter Benjamin Programme](https://www.dfg.de/en/research-funding/funding-opportunities/programmes/individual/walter-benjamin) (Germany), [SNSF Postdoc.Mobility](https://www.snf.ch/en/XIZpfY3iVS5KRRoD/funding/careers/postdoc-mobility) (Switzerland), [NSERC Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/students-etudiants/pd-np/pdf-bp_eng.asp) (Canada), and [BARD Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://www.bard-isus.com/) (Israel–US agricultural research).

        ## Postdoctoral Fellowships — US-Based Programs

        | Program | Who can apply | Support | Application |
        |---|---|---|---|
        | [NSF Postdoctoral Research Fellowships in Biology](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/prfb-postdoctoral-research-fellowships-biology) | US citizens, nationals, or permanent residents | About $110,000 per year for 24 months | Annual; the current track focuses on the intersection of AI and biology (2026 deadline: September 29) |
        | [USDA NIFA AFRI Education & Workforce Development Fellowships](https://www.nifa.usda.gov/grants/funding-opportunities/agriculture-food-research-initiative-education-workforce-development) | US citizens, nationals, or permanent residents; agriculture-relevant research (our work qualifies) | See the current program announcement | Annual, typically due in spring |
        | [Katherine Esau Postdoctoral Fellowship](https://www.plb.ucdavis.edu/katherine-esau-fellowship) | Research on plant structure integrated with development, evolution, or function, with a UC Davis faculty mentor | 2 years, renewable for a third; NIH-scale salary | See the program page |
        | [UC President's Postdoctoral Fellowship Program](https://ppfp.ucop.edu/) | Recent PhDs interested in a UC research career | Fellowship plus incentives for later UC faculty hiring | Annual |

        ## Graduate Fellowships — US Citizens and Permanent Residents

        | Program | Who can apply | Support | Application |
        |---|---|---|---|
        | [NSF Graduate Research Fellowship (GRFP)](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/) | US citizens, nationals, or permanent residents. As of 2026, you must apply as an undergraduate or first-year graduate student | 5-year fellowship with 3 years of stipend and tuition support | Annual, fall deadlines. Plan early — the eligibility window is narrow |
        | [USDA NIFA AFRI Predoctoral Fellowships](https://www.nifa.usda.gov/grants/funding-opportunities/agriculture-food-research-initiative-education-workforce-development) | US citizens, nationals, or permanent residents in agriculture-relevant research | See the current program announcement | Annual, typically due in spring |
        | [FFAR Fellows](https://ffarfellows.org/) | PhD students at US institutions with 3 years remaining; requires a non-federal sponsor (the Rockey FFAR Fellows track waives this) | 3-year program; sponsor and FFAR split costs | Annual |

        Computationally minded students should also look at the [DOE Computational Science Graduate Fellowship](https://www.krellinst.org/csgf), [DoD NDSEG](https://ndseg.org/), and the [Hertz Fellowship](https://www.hertzfoundation.org/).

        ## Graduate Fellowships — International Students

        | Program | Who can apply | Support | Application |
        |---|---|---|---|
        | [Fulbright Foreign Student Program](https://foreign.fulbrightonline.org/) | Apply through the Fulbright commission or US embassy in your home country | Varies by country | Typically about a year before intended start |
        | [Schlumberger Faculty for the Future](https://www.facultyforthefuture.net/) | Women from developing and emerging economies pursuing STEM PhDs or postdocs abroad | Up to $50,000 per year, renewable | Annual, applications typically September–November |
        | [AAUW International Fellowships](https://www.aauw.org/resources/programs/fellowships-grants/current-opportunities/international/) | Women pursuing graduate study in the US | $18,000–30,000 per year | Annual |
        | [Quad Fellowship](https://www.quadfellowship.org/) | STEM graduate students from participating countries | See site | Annual |
        | [Aga Khan Foundation International Scholarship](https://www.akdn.org/what-we-do/education/international-scholarships) | Students from select countries; half grant, half loan | See site | Annual |

        **Check your national scholarship agency first** — for many students this is the most direct route. Examples: [China Scholarship Council](https://www.csc.edu.cn/) (China), [Higher Education Commission](https://www.hec.gov.pk/) (Pakistan), NIIED / Kwanjeong / KFAS (Korea), ANID (Chile), [CAPES](https://www.gov.br/capes/pt-br) (Brazil). Joint-PhD and visiting-student programs (such as CSC's) can also support a 1–2 year research stay in our lab while you stay enrolled at your home institution.

        ## See something that fits?

        Email Grey at [gmonroe@ucdavis.edu](mailto:gmonroe@ucdavis.edu) with the program you have in mind and a short description of your research interests. We will talk through ideas by email and, if it looks like a good match, develop the application together.
