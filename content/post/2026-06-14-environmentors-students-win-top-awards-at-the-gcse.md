Four high school students from the UC Davis EnvironMentors chapter traveled to Panama City this June to present their environmental research at the GCSE International Science Fair.

All four students earned scholarships to attend. Anna Callens won first place overall, the fair's top award, and Bareera Rehman won Best Presentation. Marissa Bautista and Mudasser Ahmed also presented their work to an international audience.

Their projects spanned bird diversity in the Sacramento River watershed, the chemical footprint of gravel mining on a local creek, nitrogen in surface and drinking water near agricultural land, and salt tolerance in walnuts.

EnvironMentors pairs UC Davis graduate-student mentors with Woodland high schoolers for a year-long, independent research project, culminating in a campus science fair and, for selected students, the international fair. Congratulations to the students and their mentors.
